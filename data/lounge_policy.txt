
Lounge Policies:

Guest Access Policy:
Each lounge member is allowed a maximum of 2 guests per visit.

Operating Hours Policy:
The lounge operates daily from 5 AM to 11 PM.

Food and Beverage Policy:
Complimentary food and beverages are provided inside the lounge.

WiFi Policy:
Free WiFi is available for all lounge members.

Entry Policy:
Members must show a valid boarding pass to enter the lounge.

Children Policy:
Children under 5 years of age can enter the lounge free of charge.
