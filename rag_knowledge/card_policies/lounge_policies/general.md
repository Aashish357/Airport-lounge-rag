Airport lounges provide seating, food, and Wi-Fi.
Access is granted based on card eligibility or payment.
Some lounges are restricted to specific terminals.
# Lounge Access Limits & Duration Policy

## Capacity Management
Lounges operate at maximum capacity limits. Real-time occupancy is monitored.

If occupancy exceeds 90% of total capacity, new bookings are temporarily suspended until space becomes available.

## Standard Access Duration
Lounge access is typically granted for 3–4 hours before scheduled flight departure.

## Extended Stays
If a flight is delayed, lounge access duration is automatically extended based on the updated departure time.

## Booking Time Restriction
Lounge access can be booked up to 24 hours before scheduled departure.

## Flight Already Departed
Lounge booking is not allowed if the flight has already departed.
